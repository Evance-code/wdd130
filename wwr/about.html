Van Rafting Adventures

At Van Rafting Adventures, we are committed to providing exhilarating rafting experiences and unforgettable moments. Our mission is to bring adventure to all while ensuring safety and fun for everyone.

Founded by rafting expert Brother Evance Katanga, Van Rafting Adventures began as a tight-knit team of whitewater enthusiasts sharing their passion for river adventures. What started with just a few rafts has grown into a globally recognized company, known for its unwavering commitment to safety, expert guides, and unforgettable experiences.

Our History

With over 50 professionally trained staff and 10,000+ successful trips, we've stayed true to our roots while expanding to offer thrilling adventures for all skill levels—from first-time floaters to adrenaline-seeking experts.

At the heart of our success is Bro Evance's belief that rafting is about more than rapids—it's about connection, challenge, and the joy of the journey. Today, we continue to honor that vision, blending cutting-edge safety standards with the same passion that inspired our founding.
