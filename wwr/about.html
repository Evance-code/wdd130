Our History

Founded by rafting expert Brother Evance Katanga, Van Rafting Adventures began as a tight-knit team of whitewater enthusiasts sharing their passion for river adventures. What started with just a few rafts has grown into a globally recognized company, known for its unwavering commitment to safety, expert guides, and unforgettable experiences. With over 50 professionally trained staff and 10,000+ successful trips, we’ve stayed true to our roots while expanding to offer thrilling adventures for all skill levels—from first-time floaters to adrenaline-seeking experts. .

At the heart of our success is Bro Evance’s belief that rafting is about more than rapids—it’s about connection, challenge, and the joy of the journey. Today, we continue to honor that vision, blending cutting-edge safety standards with the same passion that inspired our founding. Every trip with Van Rafting Adventures carries the spirit of adventure that first brought our team together, inviting you to be part of our story on the water.
